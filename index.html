Hi, I am a PhD student researching human-in-the-loop learning algorithms for agents operating in high-dimensional, interactive environments. My work addresses learning efficiency and robustness challenges by integrating probabilistic modelling and deep reinforcement learning. My aim is to enable robots to learn robust visuomotor policies for dexterous manipulation and dynamic control in the real world.

Looking forward, I want to continue working in this area through opportunities in industry research labs or post-doc offerings.
